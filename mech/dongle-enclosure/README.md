# Dongle enclosure

Two-part shell for the Rev1.1 dongle PCB (`pcb/xbx-nrf-dongle/`) with the
Kyocera FPC antenna stuck inside the lid beside the board. Generated by
`dongle_enclosure.py`; change parameters there, not in the outputs.

```
FreeCADCmd dongle_enclosure.py
```

Writes `dongle-enclosure.FCStd` (all parts plus the PCB, in assembly
position), `dongle-enclosure.step`, and `stl/` (one file per part, already in
print orientation). `dongle-pcb.step` is exported from KiCad on each run and
used for a fit check: the script prints any overlap between a part and the
board.

Outside: 58.8 × 55.0 × 12.8 mm. The outline in the PCB's `User.Comments`
grows by the rear wall (the PCB edge is the inside of the wall) and on the
right so the antenna zone fits inside the wall.

## Parts

| STL | Material | Orientation |
|---|---|---|
| `top_shell` | Any | Lid on the bed |
| `bottom_shell` | Any | Floor on the bed |
| `button` | Any | Top on the bed |
| `pipe_blue`, `pipe_red`, `pipe_green` | Clear, 100 % infill | Top on the bed |

All features suit a 0.4 mm nozzle. Thinnest: 0.8 mm (switch recess wall,
flanges), Ø2.0 mm (green light pipes, cap stems).

## Hardware

- 4 × ruthex M2 heat-set inserts (M2 × 4, Ø3.2 mm hole), pressed into the top
  shell bosses.
- 4 × M2 × 6 socket head screws, from below.

## Assembly

1. Press the inserts into the top shell.
2. Push the light pipes and button caps into the lid from inside; their
   flanges sit under the lid.
3. Stick the antenna into the lid pocket, cable towards the module's u.FL.
4. Plug the antenna into the module, place the PCB in the bottom shell, lower
   the top shell over it and screw from below. The PCB is clamped between the
   bosses at H1/H2.
